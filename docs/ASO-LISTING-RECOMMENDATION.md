## English (en-US)

### App title
Dalilna — Yemen Directory

Length: 25 / 30 characters.

### Short description
Find shops, local services, jobs and buy-and-sell listings across Yemen.

Length: 72 / 80 characters.

### Full description

Dalilna — Yemen Directory helps you find shops, local services, places, jobs and buy-and-sell listings in one app.

Search by business name, activity or service. Choose a governorate and district, then review available contact details and map information. You can enable nearby search after granting location permission.

What you can do with Dalilna
• Browse shops, stores, restaurants, bakeries, pharmacies, schools, services and public facilities.
• Search by name, category, governorate and district.
• Find nearby places when you choose to share your device location.
• Explore the buy-and-sell marketplace and submit product listings or requests. Submissions are reviewed before publication.
• Browse job opportunities and application links when listings are available.
• Review indicative prices for selected product categories.
• Use the local assistant, or enable the optional cloud assistant after consent.
• Save favourites and selected preferences on your device.

Important information
Data coverage and freshness vary by area and source. A business or listing appearing in the directory does not mean Dalilna has physically verified its activity, phone number, prices or opening hours. Contact the business to confirm details before visiting or buying.

Dalilna does not process marketplace payments in the app. Contact the seller directly and agree on the price, payment method and delivery before completing a transaction.

You can browse the directory without creating a central account. Some features require an internet connection, and nearby search may require location permission. Favourites and selected preferences are saved locally on your device.

## ASO implementation notes

- The standard main listing has no standalone keyword metadata field. Play Console separately supports custom store listings targeted to selected search keywords. Use the phrases naturally in the main title and descriptions; create keyword-targeted custom listings only when there is a clear audience or country-specific message. Do not paste the keyword list as a keyword-stuffed paragraph.
- Keep the title and short description within the character counts shown above.
- Preserve the data-coverage, business-verification and payment disclosures. Do not imply that every business is verified, that every district has complete records, or that the app processes payments.
- The proposal is not uploaded to Google Play. Approval and submission remain a separate owner decision.
