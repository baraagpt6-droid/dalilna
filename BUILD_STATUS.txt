Dalilna 1.7.0 — Build and deployment status
===========================================

- PWA + Android WebView. Android build is debug-signed for testing, not Google Play production.
- Live business search uses Geoapify Places and OpenStreetMap data. Coverage can be incomplete or out of date.
- Larger governorate areas are divided into bounded Geoapify search rectangles with a fixed tile limit.
- Community listings use PostgreSQL. All submissions remain pending until admin review.
- Approved listings are visible across devices; public lists refresh every two minutes while the app is visible.
- Product subtypes include phones, watches, cars, motorcycles, land, real estate, electronics, appliances, furniture, clothes, jewelry, honey, coffee, spices, livestock and other goods.
- Jobs with expired deadlines are hidden. External job boards are linked at source when no documented API exists.
- Marketplace API requires DATABASE_URL and db/schema.sql. Server moderation requires DALILNA_ADMIN_TOKEN.
- Render free Postgres expires after 30 days and has no backups. Use persistent storage for real listings.
- Client, merchant and local admin accounts remain device-local and are not identity verification.
- A production Android release requires a private signing keystore and field testing.
