Dalilna 2.3.0
Application release: PWA + Android WebView
Android versionCode: 230
Web app version: 2.3.0

Directory search:
- A non-empty main search loads and searches all 22 governorate catalogue files, without requiring a governorate filter.
- Selecting a category searches that category across Yemen; governorate and district scope filters remain optional.
- Arabic search normalization folds Alif variants, ta marbuta/ha, ya/alif maqsura, removes diacritics and strips the leading definite article from search tokens.
- The national catalogue is the 2026-11-22 OpenStreetMap snapshot: 14,886 records across 22 governorates and 335 districts.
- Catalogue coverage is limited to mapped and named records. A missing result does not prove that a business exists nowhere.

Marketplace:
- Approved user-submitted sale, wanted, barter, free and rental listings continue to come from the shared PostgreSQL marketplace.
- The Souq screen also shows a separate directory of commercial locations recorded in the OpenStreetMap catalogue, filterable by governorate and business category.
- A mapped business is not a claim that a product is currently in stock or available at a given price. Verified product inventory must come from a merchant feed or an approved listing.
- New listings remain pending until server-side admin approval. Shared marketplace API secrets remain server-only.

Exchange rates and gold:
- Current buy/sell indicators load from the public ye-rial.com REST API.
- Sana'a and Aden markets are shown separately because they use different Yemeni banknote editions.
- The app displays rates only when the source's last_verified_at or last_sync is within 24 hours and the required USD, SAR and 21K gold readings are present.
- If the source cannot confirm a recent reading, values are hidden rather than reusing device-stored numbers.
- Source attribution: https://ye-rial.com/ and API documentation: https://ye-rial.com/api/docs/.
- Rates are indicative market data, not an official central-bank notice or a guaranteed exchange quote.

Caching and Android:
- PWA service-worker cache name is versioned to 2.3.0; old Dalilna PWA caches are deleted on activation.
- Android version upgrades clear the WebView HTTP cache once per new version. User localStorage and user-created listings are preserved.
- Android assets are synchronized from the PWA files during CI before the APK build.
- Android APK is a debug-signed testing build, not a Google Play production release.
