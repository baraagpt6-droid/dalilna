Dalilna 1.7.0
Application release: PWA + Android WebView
Android versionCode: 164
Live place discovery: Geoapify Places and OpenStreetMap with provider attribution.
Shared marketplace: PostgreSQL API; every community submission remains pending until server-side review.
Product types include phones, watches, computers, cars, motorcycles, land, property, electronics, appliances, furniture, clothing, jewelry, honey, coffee, spices, livestock and other goods.
Approved listings are shared across devices and refreshed while the app is visible.
DATABASE_URL and DALILNA_ADMIN_TOKEN must be configured as server environment secrets and the database schema must be applied.
