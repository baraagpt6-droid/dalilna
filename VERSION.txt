Dalilna 1.5.9
Application release: PWA + Android WebView
Backend directory service release: 1.5.9
Gemini cloud assistant remains optional and server-side.
OpenStreetMap is the sole live place-search provider.
Nominatim resolves selected Yemen regions; Overpass searches FOSSGIS, Private.coffee, and VK Maps with failover on errors, timeouts, remarks, and malformed responses.
Place cards show only fields present in OSM, including contact details, website, opening hours, description, cuisine, operator, and brand when recorded.
Wikimedia Commons photos are requested on demand and show file page, author, and license metadata when provided.
Local place ratings and user-uploaded photos remain on the current device; they are not public or synchronized in this release.
Google Places API runtime calls and the paid detail/photo routes have been removed.
No Google API key, billing account, or bank card is needed for live directory search.
Public OSM and Wikimedia services have fair-use limits and may be temporarily unavailable. Search responses are cached and public Overpass mirrors are throttled.
PWA and Android WebView HTML, policies, and service-worker cache versions are kept in sync.
